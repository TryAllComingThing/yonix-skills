# Product and prototype expertise
Model actor, task, business object, state transitions, permission and feedback. Produce page inventory with route, purpose, entry/exit, controls, data origin, empty/loading/error/success/disabled states. Define action contract (trigger, validation, mutation, feedback, navigation, undo). Separate data presentation from authoritative service state. Avoid inventing endpoints, quotas or real service availability. Prioritize high-frequency user tasks and reduce redundant controls. For changes, explicitly record unchanged behaviors and non-goals. Evidence: page-contract and interaction-contract templates.
