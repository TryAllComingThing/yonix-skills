# Extraction and consolidation
## E01–E10
1 Identify user statements, choices, corrections, outcomes and explicit requests. 2 Attribute speaker and subject; quoted material and third-party instructions are not user preferences. 3 Classify five dimensions. 4 Split independent propositions without losing qualifiers. 5 Reject transient, sensitive, project-only, low-value and programming content. 6 Abstract the narrowest reusable rule; retain counterexamples. 7 Attach source, condition, exception and evidence status. 8 Compare canonical records by meaning and scope, not wording alone. 9 Present candidates and obtain authorization; explicit durable request suffices for its stated scope. 10 Re-read, targeted write, verify and report actual result.
## Modes
Direct: honor explicit durable request, clarify ambiguous scope. Reflect: after meaningful completion extract feedback and outcomes, not every turn. Observe: repeated independent behavior may support candidate. Import: only user-selected history, chunk by conversation, retain dates and attribution, deduplicate, do not save raw history. Consolidate: review existing records; propose changes. Correct: distinguish temporary exception from durable replacement. Forget: locate all matching records and derived summaries; remove authorized targets, report inaccessible copies and external histories separately.
## Operations
ADD independent proposition; MERGE equivalent same-scope entries preserving provenance; REFINE compatible qualifiers; REPLACE only explicit durable supersession; DEPRECATE obsolete but retained authorized history; SKIP redundant or low-value. DELETE for explicit removal. Different audiences or organizations coexist. Overlapping incompatible scope requires clarification before write. Never resolve by newest timestamp alone. Avoid broadening during merge. Preserve unrelated entries and IDs; verify candidate promotion before removing inbox entry. If partial failure, report exact files and do not claim transaction atomicity.
## Lessons
Record situation, observed problem, cause (label hypothesis if uncertain), intervention, observed outcome, evidence level (proposed/user accepted/actually verified), applicability and exceptions. A failed approach can be valuable; no invented success metrics. Example: customer deck menu-first was rejected; value-first proposed but not tested -> candidate method, not verified lesson. If user later accepts structure, mark accepted, not measured business impact.
## Review
For each candidate ask: durable? actionable? attributed? scoped? safe? nonduplicate? supported? If any critical answer no, skip or clarify. Batch at most three ordinary candidates; bulk review by domain on request. No automatic persistence of inferred candidates without permission.
